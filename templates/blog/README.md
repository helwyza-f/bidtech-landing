# Blog Templates

Template website untuk blog dengan article listing, search, dan navigasi kategori.

## Fitur Umum

- Article/post listing
- Article detail pages
- Search functionality
- Category/tag navigation
- Author information
- Comments section
- Related articles
- Newsletter subscription
- Social sharing

## Template yang Tersedia

- **Template 1**: Modern blog layout
- **Template 2**: Magazine style
- **Template 3**: Minimalist blog design
